Experiment-125

Information

We didn't create this abomination; we didn't know it existed. This tall, slim, white creature with those dark teeth is something that we NEVER expected. We don't know how it came here.

We know that it is highly aggressive. And it seems like it doesn't like being observed. When a scientist observed it from the observation room, after 30 minutes, the scientist died for an unknown reason; there were no traces of the creature or broken reinforcement glass. We haven't tested whether looking at it in a picture, for example, will trigger it.

Status: ALIVE

When a containment breach occurred:

  • 20/4/2024 at 11:30, it has breached the containment and killed over 20 researchers and 1 guard. Those who were injured suffered nightmares that affected them badly.
